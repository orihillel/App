Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Surfcast is a surf-forecast app. This page explains, plainly, what it collects, why, and what it never does. If something here is unclear, contact replace-with-your-email@example.com.

What stays on your device

By default, everything Surfcast remembers about you stays in your browser's local storage and never reaches any server we run: your go-to spot, any spots you've added, your alerts, your unit preference (feet/mph vs. metres/kph), your surfer profile (board and skill level), and your session log (the days you've logged, with your rating and any note). Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data deletes all of it.

Location, if you use "Best nearby"

If you tap "Best nearby," your browser asks you for permission to share your location, the same standard prompt any website would show. Your coordinates are sent, on that request only, to Open-Meteo (the weather service this app is built on) and to our own backend to fetch tide and buoy readings near you. We do not store your location, log it, or associate it with any account. If you decline the permission, this feature simply doesn't work — nothing else in the app needs your location.

If you sign in with Google or Meta (optional)

Signing in is entirely optional and exists for one reason: to sync your saved spots, alerts and preferences across your devices. If you choose to sign in:

  • We receive your name and profile picture from Google or Meta. We do not request or store your email address, even on providers that offer it.
  • We store your name, picture, and the app data above (spots, alerts, units) under an identifier tied to your provider account — not to any other identity we hold.
  • This data lives in Cloudflare's storage (the infrastructure our backend runs on) and is used for nothing except serving it back to your own signed-in devices.

You can ask us to delete your account and everything stored under it at any time by emailing replace-with-your-email@example.com from the address associated with your login.

If you turn on push notifications (optional)

If you enable an alert with push notifications, your browser generates a push subscription (an endpoint address and encryption keys — not personal information) which we store alongside the alert conditions you set (spot, minimum wave height, lead time), so a scheduled check can notify you when they're met. Turning push off, or deleting the alert, deletes this record.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifiers.
  • No cookies — the app uses local storage on your own device instead.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party services this app talks to

Surfcast is built on public weather and tide data. Fetching it means your device's coordinates (never anything more) are sent to the services below when you view a forecast:

  • Open-Meteo — wave, wind and marine forecast data.
  • NOAA / NDBC (via our own backend) — real tide predictions and live buoy readings, for spots near a US station.
  • Google / Meta — only if you choose to sign in, to verify who you are.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the backend that proxies the above and stores account data for people who sign in or enable push.

Children

Surfcast is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

If this changes in a way that affects what we collect or why, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
